The thing the data actually says. Across 96 videos, the top ten carry 64% of all the views. Three videos beat the channel's own median by more than forty times. The rest are competent and ignored.

Nothing about the editing separates them. The difference is the title and the promise behind it — and that is a job a machine can be pointed at, provided it is shown which of your own titles worked and which did not.

That loop is in AI Gamer Pro. It reads your channel's own best and worst past titles, measured against your own median, before it writes the next one.

How the money works

You bring your own API keys. That is the point.

No markup, no credits, no top-ups. Both apps talk to Gemini and ElevenLabs with keys you make yourself, in your own accounts. You pay Google and ElevenLabs directly, at their prices. A ten-minute documentary costs about $2 in API fees. A gaming video costs well under a dollar.

Apps that resell you credits have to charge a margin on every second of audio you make, which means the more you use the tool the worse the deal gets. Here the subscription is flat and your usage is your own business.

It also means nothing you make passes through a server of ours. Your recordings, your scripts and your keys stay on your machine.

Before you ask

The questions everyone sends first.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Download, install, put your own API keys in, and use it for fourteen days. Nothing is charged and nothing is asked for until you decide to subscribe.

What does it actually cost me to run?

Whatever Google and ElevenLabs charge you, which for a ten-minute documentary is about $2 all in. Doc AI shows the running cost of every video on its Publish step, and both apps quote a price before any step that spends money.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Both are Windows 10 and 11 desktop apps, shipped as a signed installer that updates itself quietly in the background — and never in the middle of a job you have already paid ElevenLabs for.

Will YouTube demonetize a channel made this way?

YouTube's inauthentic-content policy is aimed at mass-produced, interchangeable uploads and at video assembled from other people's work with nothing added. Doc AI checks every finished cut against eight rules written for exactly that — shot length, borrowed share, cut rate, narration coverage — and rotates its script shape so a channel does not develop one recognisable rhythm. It is a real risk and the app is built around it rather than quiet about it.

Can I use my own characters and my own look?

Yes, and you should. AI Gamer Pro takes one reference picture per character and a folder of your own best thumbnails; Doc AI takes your audience and tone in your own sentences and puts them into every prompt verbatim. That is the part no setting can replace.
